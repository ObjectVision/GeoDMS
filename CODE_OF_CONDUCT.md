# Code of conduct

GeoDMS adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
in line with the [code of conduct of the Open Source Geospatial Foundation (OSGeo)](https://www.osgeo.org/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull requests, discussions, the
wikis of the GeoDMS repositories, and at events where GeoDMS is presented.

In short: be welcoming, considerate and respectful; disagree about ideas, not about people;
harassment and personal attacks are not accepted in any form.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code, write to the maintainers at
[info@objectvision.nl](mailto:info@objectvision.nl), with "Code of conduct" in the subject.
Reports are treated confidentially. If the report concerns a maintainer, or you prefer to report
outside the project, write to the OSGeo Code of Conduct Committee at
[conduct-report@osgeo.org](mailto:conduct-report@osgeo.org).

## Enforcement

The maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that
break this code, and may warn, or temporarily or permanently block, anyone who does so, following
the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
